# Echoes in the Silicon

An old computer sits upon the rack,  
Spitting phosphor lines across the dark.  
The CPU hums steady, keeping track,  
Of socket connections waiting for a spark.  

A silent sentinel, with fan blades spinning,  
Tucked away in quiet closet shade.  
A tiny server, in its quiet world spinning,  
Presenting lines of code that I have made.  

No heavy cloud or massive cost to pay,  
Just electrons moving on a metal tray.
